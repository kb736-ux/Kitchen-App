Privacy policy

Last updated: April 5, 2026. This policy describes how Sheek (“we,” “us”) handles information when you use our services.

Sheek provides a manager web experience and a mobile app for restaurant teams. By using Sheek, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the services.

Information we collect

  • Account and profile. Such as name, email address, and optional profile details you or your organization provide when signing up or inviting users.
  • Organization content. Data your restaurant enters in the product (for example schedules, tasks, messages, recipes, inventory-related entries, and similar operational records).
  • Device and technical data. Such as device type, OS version, app version, and diagnostic or log data needed to operate and secure the service.
  • Notifications. If you enable notifications, we may process a device push token so we can deliver alerts you request.
  • Photos and media (optional). If you choose to upload a profile photo or similar content, we process that content to provide the feature.
  • Payment-related information (web). Subscription or checkout for paid plans may be handled by our payment processor (for example Stripe). We do not store full card numbers on our servers; the processor handles card data according to its own terms and privacy policy.

How we use information

We use information to provide, maintain, and improve Sheek; authenticate users; operate features you use; send transactional or service-related messages; protect security and integrity; comply with law; and communicate with you about support or important updates.

How we share information

We share information with service providers who help us run the product (for example cloud hosting, authentication, database, analytics, email, and payment processing), subject to appropriate safeguards. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention

We retain information as long as needed to provide the services and for legitimate business purposes (including security and legal obligations). You may request deletion or correction where applicable by contacting us (see below).

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Sheek is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and revise the “Last updated” date.

Delete your account

To request deletion of your account and related personal data, follow the steps on our delete account page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: Sheek support.
