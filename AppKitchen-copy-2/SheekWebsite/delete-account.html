Delete your account

You can request deletion of your Sheek account and personal data associated with it. Restaurant organizations may require manager approval for some records; we will confirm what applies to your request.

How to request deletion

  1. Email us from the address tied to your Sheek account.
  2. Use the subject line: Account deletion request.
  3. Include your restaurant or organization name if you use Sheek with a team.

Open support — email Sheek

We will respond with next steps and may verify your identity before deleting data. Some billing or legal records may be retained only as required by law. See our privacy policy for more detail.
